# Plain English

User-facing words follow ASD-STE100 habits and Zinsser: short words, short sentences, one idea per sentence, a human on the other side.

Read `docs/conventions/words-we-use.md` before you write a label. That file owns the word map and the forbidden AI patterns. Do not copy the lists here.

## Do

- Name the action the person takes: "Look for ideas", "Make a post", "Download pack".
- Say what happened: "News wire sent nothing back. We kept going."
- Required fields in the label: "Why (required)".
- Empty states tell the next click.

## Do not

- Internal names on the default screen: Fetch, signal, gate, deep_dive, belt, clerk, fixture, radar, factory, queue.
- The nine AI-writing tricks in `docs/conventions/words-we-use.md`.
- "That's not X. That's Y." Say the second sentence only.
- Warm-ups: "Here's the thing", "Let me be clear".
- Endings that repeat the page: "In short", "At the end of the day".

Turn this off for jokes, poems, and sales-room poetry. Leave it on for the product and the SOP.
