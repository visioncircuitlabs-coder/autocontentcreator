## Forbidden AI patterns (Hassid)

Do not use these in product copy or the SOP:

1. "That's not X. That's Y." — say Y.
2. Two fragments glued: "Fast. Simple." — pick one sentence.
3. "Less a hammer, more a scalpel." — say what to do.
4. Clapping for itself: "And that matters." Delete it.
5. "It's the Excel of AI agents." — do not compare to a tool they may not know.
6. Warm-ups: "Here's the thing." Start one sentence later.
7. Always three items. Use two or five if that is the truth.
8. Fake ranges: "5 to 10 minutes." Say how long it took, or "about a minute".
9. "In short…" endings. Stop.

When you spot a new one, add a line here.
