# Make a post — week-one steps

For a content person. No training deck. Open the app and do this.

You need the app running (someone already did `docker compose up --build`, or the local API + web). Open http://localhost:3050 on your phone or laptop. How to start it: `docs/howto-run-the-demo.md`.

This is a practice Neutrogena brand. It is not Kenvue. Nothing you approve here goes onto Instagram.

## The five steps

1. **Look for ideas**  
   Tap the big button. Wait. You will see “Looking at news…” This can take about a minute.

2. **This idea**  
   You get one idea, already picked.  
   If it says **Practice idea**, live news did not give us a closer look. That is normal. Continue.

3. **Make a post**  
   Tap **Make a post**. Wait while it writes and checks claims. Pick Version 1, 2, or 3. Read “We blocked this…” if it appears. Then tap **Make the picture**. One photo, for the version you picked. Then tap **Make a short video**. About five seconds, same product. This still does not go live.

4. **Your call**  
   Type your name. Type why you said yes or no. Both are required. The buttons stay off until you fill them.  
   **Yes, keep this** or **No, do not use this**.

5. **Phone preview**  
   Check the picture, the short video, and the caption. Tap **Download pack**. That ZIP is paused. Someone still has to post it in real life.

## What good looks like

- You never needed the words “radar”, “gate”, or “deep dive”.
- Your name and why are on the decision.
- The pack downloads. The app still says it does not go live.

## If something is quiet

- “News wire sent nothing back” — ignore it unless every source is quiet.
- The button says wait a minute — you searched too often. Wait, then tap again.
- Picture missing — still download the pack. Tell whoever runs the app.

## You cannot un-post

We never posted. A yes here only files your reason. If you said yes by mistake, tell your lead. Do not treat the ZIP as live.

## Show the machine

**How it is made** is optional. Tap it if you want to see the four columns (Radar, Memory, Factory, Command). Tap a box to read what that step does, what it takes in, and what it hands on. While Look for ideas is running, a live list fills in: Google News sent N headlines, then scoring, then the closer look. You do not need this to make a post.
